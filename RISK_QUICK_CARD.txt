# Risk Factors Quick Card - Copy/Paste for Summary Docs

## High-Risk Indicators Summary

### 🔴 Critical Predictors (MUST Include)

**1. Missed Payments History**
- Delinquent avg: 2.85 | Non-delinquent avg: 2.99
- *Why:* Direct behavioral signal of payment willingness and credit reliability

**2. Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)**
- Delinquent avg: 0.306 | Non-delinquent avg: 0.297 (3% higher)
- *Why:* Indicates available discretionary income; higher DTI = financial stress

**3. Credit Utilization Rate**
- Delinquent avg: 50.7% | Non-delinquent avg: 48.9% (3.8% higher)
- *Why:* Maxed-out credit indicates financial pressure and limited payment buffer

**4. Payment Deterioration Trend**
- % showing worsening: 39% delinquent vs 21% non-delinquent
- *Why:* Early warning indicator of emerging financial distress

**5. Account Tenure**
- Delinquent avg: 9.2 years | Non-delinquent avg: 9.8 years
- *Why:* Newly opened accounts lack established payment history with lender

## ⚠️ Surprising Findings

| Finding | Data | Impact |
|---------|------|--------|
| **High earners default** | Delinquent: $113.9K income vs Non-delinquent: $107.3K | Income ≠ Protection |
| **Age irrelevant** | Only 1 year difference (47.1 vs 46.1 years) | Remove from model |
| **Loan size irrelevant** | Virtually identical ($48.4K vs $48.7K) | Amount ≠ Risk |
| **High credit scores default** | Delinquent avg: 591 vs Non-delinquent: 575 | Score is historical, not predictive |
