## Missing Data Handling Strategy

| # | Feature | Missing Data | Handling Method | Justification |
|---|---------|--------------|-----------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Income** | 39 (7.8%) | **Employment-Based Median Imputation** | Preserves income differences by employment type; avoids distorting distribution |
| 2 | **Loan_Balance** | 29 (5.8%) | **Median Imputation** | MCAR pattern; simple median maintains distribution; low missingness |
| 3 | **Credit_Score** | 2 (0.4%) | **Median Imputation** | Minimal missing (0.4%); median safe for low-impact features |

### Key Decisions:

**Income (7.8% missing):**
- Employment-based imputation preserves income disparities across employment types
- Avoids distorting the overall income distribution
- Maintains realistic income patterns by employment category

**Loan Balance (5.8% missing):**
- Missing data appears random (MCAR - Missing Completely At Random)
- Median imputation is robust and maintains statistical properties
- Retains all 471 valid records without unnecessary removal

**Credit Score (0.4% missing):**
- Only 2 records affected; minimal impact on modeling
- Simple median imputation safe and effective for small missing proportions
- No complex methods needed for negligible missingness
